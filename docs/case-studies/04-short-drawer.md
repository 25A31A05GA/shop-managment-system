# Case 4: The counter that is short by ₹330

**The situation.** At Kurnool, Counter 1, cashier Divya: bills say ₹42,110 in cash, the drawer holds ₹41,780. **Short ₹330.**

**What the engine sees.** The result ("Short ₹330") plus cancelled bills and discount percentage. It lists what it *may* mean, for example too much change given or a bill cancelled after cash was taken. It never names a cause as fact.

**What a human should do.** Ask the closing staff to recount and note a reason. Look at the next few days before drawing any conclusion. One small gap is normal; the same cashier short on four days running is a pattern.

**What could fool it.** A float not counted in, a wrong opening balance, or two cashiers sharing a drawer.

**Lesson.** A difference is a reason to look, not proof of a problem.
