# Case 7: Sona Masoori, room to raise ₹3

**The situation.** The shop sells Sona Masoori (loose) at ₹58 per kg. A sample "price walk" of nearby shops finds ₹62.

**What the engine sees.** You are **6.5% below the market**. Suggestion: **"Room to raise to ₹61"**, worth about **₹53,694 more profit a month** at today's volume. It only suggests a rise when you are at least 3% cheaper and your margin is healthy; it warns when you are 4% or more *above* the market.

**What a human should do.** Raise by ₹1 first and watch sales for a week. Do not jump to the market price.

**What could fool it.** The market prices in the sample are made up. In a real app, someone must walk the market and type real prices in.

**Lesson.** The safest profit is a small, tested move on a fast seller.
