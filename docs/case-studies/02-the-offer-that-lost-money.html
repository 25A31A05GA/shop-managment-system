Case 2: The 1+1 offer that sold out and lost money

The situation. Diwali 2025: Horlicks buy 1 get 1 free for 7 days. It sold about 1,017 extra packets. The shopkeeper remembers it as a success.

What the engine sees. The free packets cost the shop ₹1,77,640. The extra sales did not earn that back, so the net effect on profit was -₹1,71,920. Meanwhile, a plain "₹2 off per kg" on Sona Masoori rice earned a clear profit.

What a human should do. Keep the rice-style offers, rework or drop the 1+1, and check the result with the same method next festival.

What could fool it. The sample assumes sales lift by a fixed factor. In a real shop, some customers would have bought anyway, and some would have stocked up and bought less afterwards.

Lesson. "It sold out" and "it earned money" are two different sentences.
