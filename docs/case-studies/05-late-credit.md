# Case 5: ₹1.52 lakh of late credit

**The situation.** 14 customers owe the shop money on credit (khata). The owner remembers only the big names.

**What the engine sees.** Total **₹2,58,960**, of which **₹1,52,150 (59%)** is more than 30 days late. Buckets: 0 to 15 days ₹55,720 · 16 to 30 ₹51,090 · 31 to 60 ₹1,04,330 · over 60 ₹47,820. The oldest first: Kiran Kumar, ₹32,230, 74 days.

**What a human should do.** Send the reminder to the oldest first. The tool builds the WhatsApp message ("Namaste Kiran Kumar, your balance at our shop is ₹32,230. Please pay when you can. Thank you.") and the link opens WhatsApp with the text ready.

**What could fool it.** A customer who paid in cash that was not entered. Always check before sending.

**Lesson.** Polite and early beats angry and late.
