# Case 3: Which rice do customers really prefer?

**The situation.** Sona Masoori from Andhra Pradesh (Guntur) sells **63% more** than the one from Telangana (Nalgonda). Is it better?

**What the engine sees.** It checks why one sells more:
- cheaper by about 5%
- the other was out of stock 5 more days
- kept on the front shelf

Quality signals (returns, complaints, quality score) show **no meaningful difference**. Verdict: **"Sold more mostly for other reasons"**, with the sentence *"This is NOT proof that it is better quality."*

**What a human should do.** Run a fair test: same price, same shelf, both in stock, for two weeks. Then decide.

**What could fool it.** A real preference that happens to come with a lower price. The rule needs at least two other reasons and under two quality signals before it says "other reasons".

**Lesson.** Do not change a supplier on a sales number alone.
